Sense's Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Sense is an online T-shirt store based in Metro Manila, Philippines, offering simple clothing designed for everyday life. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, submit an inquiry, or place an order request. We handle personal information in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and other applicable laws.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that is reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries and process order requests. This may include:

  • Contact details: First name, last name, email address, country or region, and optional phone number submitted through our Contact page.
  • Order details: Your name and contact information, selected T-shirt, color, size, quantity, and delivery address, when provided.
  • Messages: The contents of inquiries or other communications you send us.

You do not need to create an account to use our website. We do not provide an online payment gateway or collect payment card details through our website's ordering process.

3. How We Collect Information

We receive personal information when you submit the Contact form or voluntarily send us an email. When you select "Order Now" on a product page, the website opens an email draft addressed to Sense. Clicking the button alone does not send your information to us. We receive the details you choose to include only when you send the email.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Responding to questions and inquiries.
  • Confirming product availability and order details.
  • Coordinating payment and delivery arrangements.
  • Communicating with you about orders, returns, or other concerns.
  • Maintaining appropriate business records and complying with legal obligations.
  • Maintaining the proper operation of our website.

We process personal information only for appropriate and lawful purposes, such as responding to an order request, fulfilling an order, complying with legal requirements, or pursuing legitimate interests permitted by law.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share relevant information with other parties when necessary to respond to inquiries or complete an order. Only information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose should be shared. We may also disclose personal information when required or permitted by applicable laws.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to respond to inquiries, process and document orders, address customer concerns, or meet applicable record-keeping and legal requirements.

When personal information is no longer needed, we will delete or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal obligations.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, or misuse. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please avoid sending passwords, one-time passwords, or payment card details through email or our Contact form.

8. Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you may have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your personal information is collected and processed.
  • Access your personal information.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Object to certain types of processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, subject to applicable conditions.
  • Request the erasure or blocking of personal information when legally justified.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Seek damages for unlawful processing.
  • Lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission when your privacy rights have been violated.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11. We may request reasonable verification of your identity before responding to your request. These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and limitations.

9. Children's Privacy

Sense offers clothing for a general audience. Where required by applicable law, the appropriate consent or involvement of a parent or legal guardian must be obtained before processing a child's personal information.

10. Third-Party Websites and Policy Updates

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including social media pages. These websites have their own privacy policies and practices. We do not control these websites or how they handle your personal information, so we encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website or business practices change. Any revisions will be published on this page along with the updated date.
